NIDHI PHARMACEUTICALS

Commercial pharmaceutical distributor portal engineered with modern responsive frontend, product formulation directory, and direct B2B inquiry order workflows.

Visit Live Site ↗ ← All Projects

Client Overview & Commercial Problem

Nidhi Pharmaceuticals is a growing pharmaceutical distribution and healthcare enterprise. The client required a credible, high-trust, and compliant digital web presence to showcase their medical formulations, tablets, syrups, and pharmaceutical product catalogs to regional chemists, hospitals, and medical retailers.

Previous customer communication was purely offline. They needed a zero-friction, lightning-fast digital platform that allowed retailers to search medications, inspect packaging/batch compliance details, and initiate direct commercial wholesale purchase inquiries via integrated WhatsApp and contact channels.

Architecture & Technical Delivery

  • Frontend Stack: HTML5, CSS3, Modern JavaScript (ES6+), Vanilla architecture for zero runtime dependencies and sub-second load times.
  • Responsive Healthcare UI/UX: Clean medical-grade typography, trust badges, responsive pharmaceutical product grid, and clear categorization across tablets, capsules, injectables, and syrups.
  • B2B Inquiry System: One-click WhatsApp quotation generation pre-filling product names, quantities, and pharmacy license details.
  • Performance & SEO: 98+ Google Lighthouse Performance score, semantic schema markup, and rapid accessibility across mobile networks.
